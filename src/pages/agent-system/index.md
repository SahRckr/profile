# agent-system

agent-system is a personal automation program that Sahil Satishkumar runs on his own computer, for his own Google account only. It isn't offered to anyone else, and there is no sign-up.

## What it does

agent-system sorts Sahil's inbox and plans his calendar for him. To do that, it asks Google for three permissions:

- **Gmail (read and modify):** reads newsletters, transaction alerts, one-time codes and job-funding emails; labels them, archives them and marks them read; and files what matters into Sahil's own notes.
- **Calendar events:** creates and moves Sahil's focus, leisure and time-review blocks on his own calendars.
- **Calendar list (read-only):** finds which of Sahil's calendars to read and write.

## Why it needs this data

The only purpose is to keep Sahil's inbox sorted and his calendar planned without manual work. The data isn't used for anything else, and nobody else's data is accessed.
